Zodiac Match privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Zodiac Match is a tile-matching puzzle game for children, made by Nexus Game Devs ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no chat and no way for players to share anything.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • Game progress stays on the device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, set up for children: no personalized ads, no remarketing, and the advertising identifier is not used.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play.

Information stored on the device

The game saves its progress (levels, stars, stickers, settings and what has been bought) in a file inside the app's private storage on the device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Every ad request is marked as coming from a child, with personalization turned off and the maximum ad rating set to G (general audiences). Under these settings Google does not show personalized or remarketing ads and does not use the Android advertising identifier, and the game does not ask for permission to read that identifier.

To deliver ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob receives some information automatically: the device's IP address (which can show the approximate region), basic device and app information, an app set identifier, diagnostic information, and interactions with ads. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and its policies for ads in apps for children.

Ads appear only between levels and on two menu screens, never during play and never when the game starts. Buying "Remove ads" turns them off.

Purchases (Google Play)

Extra level packs and ad removal can be bought once, through Google Play. A grown-up question appears before any purchase. Payment details are handled by Google Play, never by the game; the game only learns which products the Google account owns, to unlock them. See the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Text-to-speech

The game can say animal names with the device's own text-to-speech voice. This runs on the device; the game sends nothing anywhere for it.

Children's privacy

The game is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If the game's handling of information changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change reaches players.
